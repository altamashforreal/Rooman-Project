Job Title: Data Scientist
Company: TechNova Analytics
Location: Bangalore, India (Hybrid)
Experience Required: 3–6 years

About the Role:
TechNova Analytics is hiring a Data Scientist to join our AI/ML team. You will
design and deploy machine learning models that power our SaaS analytics platform,
used by over 500 enterprise clients. You will work closely with product managers,
engineers, and business stakeholders to translate complex data into actionable insight.

Key Responsibilities:
- Build, train, and deploy machine learning models for classification, regression,
  and time-series forecasting tasks.
- Work with large structured and semi-structured datasets using Python, SQL, and Spark.
- Design and run A/B experiments; analyse results and communicate findings clearly.
- Collaborate with MLOps to productionise models (Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines).
- Mentor junior data scientists and participate in code reviews.
- Write clear technical documentation and present results to non-technical stakeholders.

Required Skills and Qualifications:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related field.
- 3+ years of hands-on experience in data science or machine learning.
- Strong Python skills: numpy, pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib/seaborn.
- Experience with at least one deep learning framework: TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Solid understanding of statistics: hypothesis testing, distributions, Bayesian thinking.
- Experience with SQL and relational databases (PostgreSQL or MySQL).
- Familiarity with cloud platforms: AWS (SageMaker, S3, EC2) or GCP (Vertex AI, BigQuery).
- Strong communication and stakeholder presentation skills.

Preferred (Nice-to-Have):
- Experience with NLP (transformers, LLMs, Hugging Face).
- Knowledge of MLflow, DVC, or similar experiment tracking tools.
- Experience with Apache Spark or Databricks.
- Contributions to open-source projects or published research.
- Experience in a SaaS or product company environment.
